# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React and Vite, retained from the repository example. No deployment target specified.

## Users

Confirmed primary audience: self-guided students. Vietnamese interface and lessons follow the supplied Vietnamese curriculum; language remains an inferred product choice.

## Product Purpose

Make a 14-lesson astronomy curriculum approachable through visual exploration, short lesson previews, and learner-controlled progress.

## Positioning

The curriculum follows the supplied Vietnamese lesson outline from sky observation to the origin and future of the universe.

## Operating Context

Learners browse on web and mobile, choose a topic, and resume their own sequence. Lesson titles and topic lists come from the supplied curriculum image.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current prototype includes accent-insensitive lesson search, topic filters, introductory lesson readings, quick quizzes, a Kepler orbit simulation, local progress tracking, and optional TideCloak authentication. Content beyond the supplied topic outline is introductory material with further-reading links to NASA, CERN, and the US Naval Observatory. It is a starter course, not a complete textbook.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied screenshot contains 14 lesson titles and topic lists. NASA imagery is used with source links and image credit in the project. No user accounts or persisted server data exist.

## Product Principles

- Let astronomical images lead into concepts.
- Keep the Vietnamese curriculum recognizable and complete.
- Let learners move at their own pace.
- Keep progress clear and easy to change.
- Keep TideCloak configuration out of source control and scope local progress by the authenticated subject.
